They say a blank page is the universe's way of telling you that you should be doing something else.

Murphy's Law says that anything that can go wrong, will. It applies perfectly to this blog: every time I think about writing something, a bug shows up in production, the server crashes, or everything just goes wrong like it does every Friday.

**Something will be here soon.** Web dev, mobile, AI, Claude Code, insane stuff I discover at 2am — that kind of content. Maybe also why setting up GitHub Pages is harder than it should be. Mostly I'm just trying to claw my way out of dev hell one deploy at a time.

Stay tuned. Or don't. I'll be back either way.
